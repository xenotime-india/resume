Shopping app for North America's largest headwear retailer: browsing, checkout and the Access Pass loyalty programme on iOS and Android from one React Native codebase. I built the Heroku microservices that bridge the legacy commerce backend to the mobile front end, so the app shipped without re-platforming the backend.
