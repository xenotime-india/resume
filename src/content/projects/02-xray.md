Org health and metadata analysis tool for Salesforce architects. Scanners crawl an org through the Metadata API, score the depth and breadth of customisation, and generate PDF and Google Sheets reports that turn technical debt into a prioritised list. Built as a Node.js service with Apex-side collectors.
